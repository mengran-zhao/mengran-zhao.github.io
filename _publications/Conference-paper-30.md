To address the limitation of resolution performance caused by the high spatial correlation of radiation fields in radar coincidence imaging (RCI), this paper proposes a measurement mode selection method based on frame potential minimization. By incorporating a Gram matrix update strategy, the proposed method enables the efficient selection of an optimal subset from a massive pool of candidate measurement modes. Numerical experiments demonstrate that the proposed approach effectively enhances the system resolution.
