In this paper, a super-resolution microwave coincidence imaging (MCI) method based on a Squeeze-and-Excitatoin Convolutional Neural Network (SE-CNN) is proposed. The proposed SE-CNN employs a modified Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization algorithm to extract amplitude and phase features of the radiation field and is trained on a two-channel dataset incorporating multi-target interference and ambient noise. It dynamically weights feature channels to predict the primary target’s position, with the complex scattering coefficient inverted via least-squares. Numerical simulations demonstrate 10-fold super-resolution in distinguishing targets within the 3 dB beamwidth at a 20 dB signal-to-noise ratio, achieving high accuracy and robustness under strong interference.
